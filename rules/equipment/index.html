Equipment

Gear is a single, sprawling chapter in the CRB — one of the most complete in the current draft, even though it isn't yet numbered into the book's own chapter sequence. Below is a shorthand tour of what's in it, not the full catalog.

Everything in NYTE City has a price. That's not philosophy — it's the operating principle of the place. This is the only equipment chapter in the book, and it covers everything: Weapons, Armor, Cybernetics, Equipment, and Vehicles.

The economy: Çredits, Skrip, and the TAG

NYTE City runs on two currencies at once. Çredits (Ç) are the digital, tracked currency of the legitimate economy — corporate payroll, licensed vendors, anything that runs through the LINK. Skrip ($) is untraceable physical currency, at rough parity with Çredits, and the preferred medium for anyone conducting business they'd rather not see logged.

Every resident carries a TAG — a Trusted Authentication Gateway, the government-issued digital identity behind the city's Universal Basic Income. It comes with a tradeoff: the city always knows where a TAG is. Going TAGless means privacy, at the cost of the UBI and life entirely inside the skrip economy.

Weapons

Every weapon entry shares the same read: Skill, Damage, Style (Blade or Blunt), Reach, Availability, Cost, Tags, and Features — plus type-specific columns like Accuracy, Range, Rate of Fire, and Capacity for ranged weapons. Categories run from Handguns and SMGs up through Rifles, Heavy Weapons, Melee, and Martial Arts weapons, with a full ammunition and weapon-modification system (Silencers, Scopes, SMART Link targeting, and more) layered on top.

Armor

Every piece of armor is defined by three numbers and how you got it:

StatWhat it does
PROTA die rolled on every hit, subtracted from incoming damage of any type.
RESFlat damage reduction against matching damage types (Blade, Blunt, Ballistic by default).
INTAn integrity pool that wears down as you take hits. At 0, the armor is Compromised — PROT still rolls, but RES stops applying until it's repaired.

Example — Reinforced Denim Vest (Light Coverage, Common, 150Ç): 1d4 PROT, +4 RES, 10 INT. The baseline: easy to conceal, easy to replace, stops what it can reach and nothing aimed anywhere else.

Vehicles

Vehicles fall into two categories: Grounders (wheeled or hover-based surface transport) and Jumpers (aerial vehicles working the city's vertical traffic grid). Vehicles use the same PROT / RES / INT framework as personal armor, plus Speed, Seats, and Mod slots.

Example — Crotchrocket (Common, 2,000Ç): 2 seats, 1d4 PROT, 2 RES, 50 INT, 120 km/h, 1 mod slot. Fast, cheap, and exactly as fragile as it sounds.

Cybernetics

Chips, mods, implants, and the other modifications you can make to your body — referenced throughout this chapter (a SMART Link neural interface, a Cybernetics Maintenance Kit for field repairs) but detailed in full in its own Cybernetics chapter, still being written.
