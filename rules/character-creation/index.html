Character Creation

Character creation in Shadows takes you from a blank sheet to someone who belongs in NYTE City. Below is the manuscript's own walkthrough of that flow — the same text that's in the current CRB draft, not a paraphrase of it.

Your character matters. NYTE City might not notice their passing, but the table does. Character creation asks you to bring someone into being, define who they were, and decide who they might become. A character sheet will help — grab one if you have it. Everything in this section has a place on it.

Character Creation takes time. Pause when you need to. Nothing locks in until you're ready.

The general flow

  1. Determine the Campaign Power Level with your GM.
  2. Decide who you want to be in NYTE City.
  3. Roll for your Stat Points and assign your Base Stats — this represents the person you were before power found you.
  4. Choose your Archetype — the pivot that sharpens who you are becoming.
  5. Build your character's history: what shaped them? What do they owe, fear, or want?
  6. Allocate Skill Points to reflect your training and expertise.
  7. Spend Character Points on Advantages, Disadvantages, and powers. Allocate any remaining points.

What a character is

The character you create is your life in NYTE City. You decide what they do, what they say, and how they respond when the world pushes back. They can be anyone — a new name, a new history, a version of yourself with different scars. The only requirement is that you commit to them fully. NYTE City changes people. The character you start with won't be the one you end up with.

Rolling Stat Points

Time to find out what you're working with. Roll 3d10 + 30.

Use these points to allocate to your base Stats — BOD, REF, MOB, INT, TECH, COOL, MAG, EMP — which represent your starting point as a Human, before pivoting down one of the Archetype paths. Every stat starts at a base of 1; 10 is your stat maximum.

For each point below 4, you take a −1 penalty to that stat for skills or synergies. For each point above 6, you gain a +1 bonus. Pro tip: starting below 4 creates penalties — do it intentionally.

Derived Attributes

Once your Base Stats are set, calculate TOL, WILL, and SAN. These are floors — no effect can reduce them below this base.

AttributeFormula
Tolerance (TOL)Base 1 + INT Modifier + COOL Modifier + EMP Modifier
Will (WILL)Base 1 + BOD Modifier + INT Modifier + EMP Modifier
Sanity (SAN)EMP × 10 (max 95%)

With your stats in place, the next question is what NYTE City made you — your Archetype.
