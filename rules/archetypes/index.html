Archetypes

Everyone starts human. What happens next is up to you. An Archetype is how your character survives NYTE City: the power they start with, and the Milestones that unlock as they grow.

Where everyone starts: human

Most humans don't know vampires, werewolves, nephilim, and demons walk among them.

Humans are the most common sentient beings in NYTE City, in every profession at every level. Every character begins as one. The five Archetypes below are the ways a person stops being ordinary: three of them stay human and close the gap on their own terms, and two left humanity behind before your story begins.

Mortal paths

Still human. Trained, augmented, or taught to speak to the {% include term.html id="aether" %} until they can stand in a room with something that isn't.

Professional

{{ site.data.archetypes.professional.hook }}

Humans who trained until they can compete with supernatural beings. They bring skills, techniques, and Tweaks into situations that expect a mundane human, and teach those situations otherwise. Where others rely on supernatural gifts or tech, a Professional relies on preparation and precision. They are not the most powerful thing in any room. They are frequently the most dangerous.

Professions: Cleaner, Enforcer, Fence, Jack of All Trades, Mercenary, Slayer, True Warrior, Wheelman

Cyborg

{{ site.data.archetypes.cyborg.hook }}

Some people love technology; others become it. Cyborgs are humans who went all in: a {% include term.html id="nci" text="Neurocybernetic Interpreter" %}, cyber-limbs, nanobots, and more. They pay for it in Çredits and in {% include term.html id="humanity" %}, closing the gap between human and supernatural one install at a time. Every enhancement is a decision you don't get to walk back, and the city keeps adding to the bill.

Arcanist

{{ site.data.archetypes.arcanist.hook }}

Humans who learned to use magic. Wizards, sorcerers, mages: those who can manipulate Aether and wield it like a weapon. What they gain in power, they trade for the risk of ripping themselves, or the world, apart. Every spell is a negotiation with forces that do not care whether you survive the conversation, and the Aether leaves marks. Arcanists wear them.

Origins: Book (magic is knowledge), Blood (magic is instinct), Bound (magic is debt)

Supernatural paths

No longer human, whether they were turned, born to it, or made, and it happened before the campaign starts. They live in a version of NYTE City most people will never see.

Vampire

{{ site.data.archetypes.vampire.hook }}

Centuries to accumulate power, influence, and everything that comes with both. Vampires belong to {% include term.html id="houses" %}, each with their own strengths, resources, allies, and enemies, and a secretive {% include term.html id="unseen-court" text="Unseen Court" %} keeps their kind out of sight while it leans on corporations, syndicates, and politicians. You navigate hunger, power, and eternity in equal measure. Feeding is not optional.

Werewolf

{{ site.data.archetypes.werewolf.hook }}

Lycanthropy is both a curse and a gift: immense strength, savage instinct, and the weight of a tribe and lineage older than the city. Werewolves protect humanity from the things beyond the Veil, whether or not anyone knows it. You live between restraint and release. The beast is not a weapon you draw cleanly; it has its own opinions about how things should be resolved.

Origins: Trueborn, Unblooded, Forge Fangs
