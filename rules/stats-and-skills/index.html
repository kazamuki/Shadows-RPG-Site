Stats & Skills

Every character in Shadows is built from the same set of traits — they just tell the city how hard it has to hit before you break. Here's the shorthand version of Stats, Attributes, and how a Skill Check actually resolves at the table.

Stats

Stats describe how your body and mind function when things go wrong. Most people fall somewhere in the middle (4–6). Drop too far below that, and the world starts pushing back harder. Rise above it, and doors begin to open…sometimes dangerous ones.

BOD — Body

Health, endurance, raw physical damage. The machine you were issued at birth.

REF — Reflexes

Hand-eye coordination and reaction speed. Combat, spellcasting under pressure, any moment hesitation gets you killed.

MOB — Mobility

How fast you move when staying put stops being an option. Sets your run and sprint speed.

INT — Intelligence

Pattern recognition, argumentation, thinking three moves ahead.

TECH — Technical Aptitude

How well you understand the machines that run the city — and how badly it goes when you don't.

COOL — Cool

How you act when everything's on the line and everyone's watching. Panic is contagious. So is confidence.

MAG — Magnetism

Presence. The way a room shifts when you walk in. That's not charm — that's gravity.

EMP — Empathy

Your overall humanity, tied directly to Sanity. It's also the first thing NYTE City takes.

Attributes

Derived from your Stats, Attributes measure how you push back against NYTE City's pressure:

AttributeWhat it measures
Tolerance (TOL)How much strain your body endures before the power you wield starts pushing back — Aether, cybernetics, anything with a cost.
Will (WILL)Your defense against intrusion: mental, emotional, or supernatural.
Sanity (SAN)Your connection to reality. In Shadows, no one is completely sane.

The Skill Check

The simplest resolution in Shadows is a Skill Check. Roll 1d10 and add the Skill rank + Primary Stat + Synergy Bonus modifiers from your character sheet. The number to beat is based on the difficulty of the action, which the GM sets — typically 10 for easy, 15 for medium, 20 for hard. If you're making a Skill Check on a skill you haven't trained in, you add just the Primary Stat.

Roll a 10 and the die explodes — roll again and add the result, and keep going on another 10. Roll a 1 and it's a Botch: not only does the action fail, the situation gets worse.

Every skill is tied to a Primary Stat and a Synergy Bonus stat. Example: Acrobatics (REF / BOD) — a character with REF 8 and BOD 9 (Synergy bonus +3) rolls a flat +8 untrained, or +12 (1 + 8 + 3) once trained to Rank 1.

Skills

Skills fall into three categories — Combat, Utility, and General:

Combat

Archery, Beam Weapons, Combat Sense, Dodge, Handguns, Heavy Weapons, Martial Arts, Melee, Rifles, SMGs, Tactics.

Utility

Basic Tech, Cybernetics, Demolitions, Engineering, Interface, Medical, Programming, Robotics, Security.

General

Acrobatics, Athletics, Awareness, Deception, Disguise, Intimidation, Intuition, Investigation, Occult Lore, Persuasion, Pilot, Seduction, Stealth, Streetwise, Survival, Tracking.
