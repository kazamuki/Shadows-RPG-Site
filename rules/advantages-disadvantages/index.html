Advantages & Disadvantages

Two characters can share every Stat and still play nothing alike. This is where that difference comes from.

Nobody in NYTE City is clean. The advantages you've earned. The disadvantages you carry. Advantages cost Character Points. Disadvantages grant them. The more the city has marked you, the more you have to work with.

A few Advantages

Earned · costs points

Danger Sense
The city always telegraphs. Most people aren't listening.
Ghost TAG(s)
In NYTE City, your {% include term.html id="tag" %} is your face. This gives you a different one.
Aura Sight
Everyone in NYTE City wears a mask. This sees behind it.
Nine Lives
Most people get one chance. You get nine. The city will catch up eventually.

A few Disadvantages

Carried · grants points

Enemies
Everyone in NYTE City is someone's problem. You're several.
Monster Magnet
The supernatural doesn't find everyone. It finds you.
Cursed
Curses don't expire. They mature.
Pact
Some deals get made before you know you're at the table.

In play

Paid in full

The job paid. Doc Malin transferred the Çredits, the crew split the take, and Juno was halfway through her first drink in a week when the lights in the bar dimmed. Just slightly. Just for her.

She made the Pact at nineteen. She doesn't remember the terms, only the voice, and the voice is on the next barstool now, asking a favor. Tonight. Across the river. The crew isn't invited.

She can say no. Then the luck that's carried her through every job goes quiet, and everything she reaches for gets a little heavier, until she says yes.
