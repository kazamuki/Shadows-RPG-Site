Site changelog

What's changed here on Shadowsrpg.com, most recent first.

2026-09-07

Light mode

There's now a sun/moon toggle in the top nav for folks who prefer a light theme. Your choice is remembered on this device, so it stays put next time you visit.

2026-09-07

Try the character sheet right now

Character Creation now links straight to the real, live character sheet — build one in your browser, or grab a blank sheet to fill in by hand.

2026-09-07

Fixed the favicon and link previews

Sharing a shadowsrpg.com link now shows a proper preview image, and the browser tab icon finally shows the skull/dice mark instead of a solid black square.

2026-09-07

Cleaner path into the Rules Preview

Home's Core Rulebook section is now a proper shortcut grid into every Rules Preview page, instead of a scrolling carousel with a couple of dead-end duplicate links. The About page introduces the team by name, and you can now join us on Discord from Home or About.

2026-09-06

Shadowsrpg.com rebuilt from scratch

New Home, Rules Preview (Character Creation, Power Levels, Stats & Skills, Advantages & Disadvantages, Archetypes, Equipment), The World, About, and Credits pages — all built fresh from the current Core Rulebook manuscript instead of the old, years-stale site.
