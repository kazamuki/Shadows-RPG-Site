2026-09-06

Shadowsrpg.com is getting rebuilt

This site is being rebuilt from the ground up, page by page, straight from the current Core Rulebook manuscript instead of the old, years-stale version. Home, the Rules Preview hub, and The World are up first, with everything else following close behind.

Worth calling out: NYTE City's setting year has moved from 2079 to 2099 in the current manuscript, and Power Levels have been streamlined from seven named tiers down to four — Street Level, Heroic, Shadows, and World Coming Down.
