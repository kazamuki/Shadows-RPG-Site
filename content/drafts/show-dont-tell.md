# Show, don't tell: Session 5 copy draft

Draft for `content/messaging-plan.md` Session 5 (2026-09-26). Needs Ken's approval (and D6)
before anything goes into HTML.

## D6, as drafted

- **In-world voice**, same register as the manuscript excerpts (NYTE City narrating), checked
  against `brand/GUIDE_Shadows_Voice.md` via the voice-editor skill.
- **No numbers, no procedure.** Each scene shows what a mechanic *feels like* from inside the
  fiction; the mechanic is only named with a word the page has already introduced (a die
  "explodes", a Disadvantage "pays out"). Same altitude rule as the rest of Rules Preview.
- **New writing, so not labelled as a CRB excerpt.** Each scene gets a small "In play" label
  and its own styling (not `.manuscript-excerpt`), so nobody reads it as quoted book text.
- **One job, three pages.** All three scenes use the CRB's own running example from the GM
  Workshop (`232_Consequences and Fallout`): the **Missing Cargo** job, with Hyde Dynomat, the
  Red Hands, and Doc Malin. A reader going through Rules Preview in order meets the same job
  three times, from three angles. The characters (Rook, Juno) are new and site-only.

## 1. Stats & Skills: "The fence"

**Placement:** new section after "How a check resolves", before "Skills, at a glance".
Mirrors the CRB's own Skill Check example (the alley, the chain-link fence), taken one step
further into consequence.

> Headlights sweep the mouth of the alley. Security is thirty seconds out, and the only way
> through is a chain-link fence with razor wire on top. Rook doesn't think about it. She runs,
> plants a boot, and the die explodes. Then it explodes again.
>
> She doesn't just clear the fence. She's over, down, and gone before the first drone crests
> the rooftops, the datastick still in her fist.
>
> That's the roll. Here's the rest of it: the camera on the gate caught one frame of her face on
> the way over. Nothing happens tonight. Three days later, a Hyde Dynomat contractor starts
> asking about her at a noodle stand on Canal.
>
> The city didn't punish her. It just remembered.

## 2. Power Levels: "Same job, two scales"

**Placement:** after the four Power Level cards, before the closing "more power does not make
your character safer" excerpt (which it sets up).

> At **Street Level**, the Missing Cargo job is a van full of stolen biotech, a gang called the
> Red Hands who want it back, and a fixer named Doc Malin who's already been paid. Get it wrong
> and the Red Hands know your face. That's a bad month.
>
> At **Shadows**, it's the same van. But the biotech belongs to Hyde Dynomat, the Red Hands are
> a front for something older that doesn't want its name on the LINK, and Doc Malin is the only
> one who knows it. Get it wrong and a district goes dark, and three powers that have never
> agreed on anything agree on you.
>
> Same job. The city decides how far it echoes.

## 3. Advantages & Disadvantages: "Paid in full"

**Placement:** after the Disadvantages grid, before "The full lists run much longer".
Uses **Pact** (already one of the page's four Disadvantage cards). Its real effects (no Luck,
everything harder while you defy it) appear only as feel.

> The job paid. Doc Malin transferred the Çredits, the crew split the take, and Juno was halfway
> through her first drink in a week when the lights in the bar dimmed. Just slightly. Just for
> her.
>
> She made the Pact at nineteen. She doesn't remember the terms, only the voice, and the voice
> is on the next barstool now, asking a favor. Tonight. Across the river. The crew isn't
> invited.
>
> She can say no. Then the luck that's carried her through every job goes quiet, and
> everything she reaches for gets a little heavier, until she says yes.
>
> Disadvantages pay out at character creation. They collect later.

## Voice-editor pass (notes)

- **Working:** each scene leads with fiction and lands on a world truth ("It just remembered",
  "The city decides how far it echoes", "They collect later"). That's the guide's Truth →
  Manifestation → Inference order run backwards, which suits a short scene. Humor stays dry and
  in-world ("That's a bad month").
- **Tightened in this draft:** "Rook doesn't think" → "doesn't think about it" (reads as
  reflex, not stupidity); cut a "maybe less" hedge after "thirty seconds out"; "The city just
  decides" → "The city decides" (assert, don't soften).
- **Watch:** the Pact closer, "Disadvantages pay out at character creation", is the only line
  that points at the build process. It's deliberate, since it echoes the page's own "Disadvantages
  grant them", but it's the line to cut if it reads too mechanical.
