## Pitch section (replaces "What is Shadows?" + "What is the Synergy System?")

One centered section, reading width, no cards.

### Heading: **The myths never left. They adapted.**

NYTE City swallowed the whole Eastern Seaboard. On the surface it's everything you'd expect
from 2099: corporate towers, street gangs, and a NET that sees everything. Underneath, the old
stories are still running the place. Vampires own the nightclubs and the blood banks.
Werewolves clock in for security work and call out on certain nights. The Fae negotiate
contracts with finer print than any corporation. (They also own K-pop.)

And the city is paying attention. Steal a car, burn down a corporate front, leave a body in
the wrong alley. NYTE City won't stop you, but it will answer. Fixers remember. Gangs
retaliate. Corporations "audit." Every job leaves a mark your crew has to live with. Shadows is
for players who want, in the rulebook's own words, "danger with weight, power with cost, and
hope that has to be earned."
