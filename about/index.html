About

Get Dangerous Games, speaking for itself — no in-world voice here, just us.

Who's making Shadows

No publisher, no committee — a small crew who wanted to run a cybernetic, supernatural game, couldn't find the system they were looking for, and built one instead. The Synergy System, NYTE City, and everything on this site is the product of years of actual play at the table, rewritten and rebalanced against what real groups needed.

Creator

Deighton “d33Kode”

25+ years behind the GM screen, and the one who built Shadows from the ground up — the Synergy System, NYTE City, all of it started with him.

Game Master & Technical Writer

Scott “Melf”

Joined the crew seven years ago and fell hard for Shadows. Scott's the direct line that turns Deighton's raw ideas into the words that actually land in the CRB.

Site & Systems

Ken “Kaza”

A relative newcomer to the hobby himself — Shadows is his only full campaign — Ken's on a mission to get everyone else playing it too, one rebuilt page at a time.

Part of the Get Dangerous family

Shadows is Get Dangerous Games' flagship tabletop project, developed alongside our other work over at getdangerous.net. Same studio, same voice, different corner of the work.
