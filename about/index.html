About

Get Dangerous Games, speaking for itself — no in-world voice here, just us.

Who's making Shadows

Shadows is built by Get Dangerous Games, a small independent studio — no publisher, no committee, just people who wanted to run a cybernetic, supernatural game and couldn't find the system they were looking for, so they built one instead.

The Synergy System, NYTE City, and everything on this site is the product of years of actual play at the table, rewritten and rebalanced against what real groups needed, not what looked good on paper.

Part of the Get Dangerous family

Shadows is Get Dangerous Games' flagship tabletop project, developed alongside our other work over at getdangerous.net. Same studio, same voice, different corner of the work.
