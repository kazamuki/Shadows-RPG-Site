Shadows

A tabletop RPG where cyberpunk and the supernatural share one city, and the city keeps score. Welcome to NYTE City, 2099.

Meet NYTE City Explore the Rules

The myths never left. They adapted.

NYTE City swallowed the whole Eastern Seaboard. On the surface it's everything you'd expect from 2099: corporate towers, street gangs, and a {% include term.html id="link" text="LINK" %} that sees everything. Underneath, the old stories are still running the place. Vampires own the nightclubs and the blood banks. Werewolves clock in for security work and call out on certain nights. The Fae negotiate contracts with finer print than any corporation. (They also own K-pop.)

And the city is paying attention. Steal a car, burn down a corporate front, leave a body in the wrong alley. NYTE City won't stop you, but it will answer. Fixers remember. Gangs retaliate. Corporations “audit.” Every job leaves a mark your crew has to live with. Shadows is for players who want, in the rulebook's own words, “danger with weight, power with cost, and hope that has to be earned.”

If you've ever wanted cyberpunk and urban fantasy at the same table, this is that game.

Who you can be

Everyone starts human. What happens next is up to you: three ways to stay human and close the gap, and two ways to leave humanity behind.

How it plays

Shadows runs on the Synergy System, our own d10 system. One die resolves almost anything a scene throws at you: hacking the LINK, dropping a threat, casting a spell, or talking your way out of a holding cell.
