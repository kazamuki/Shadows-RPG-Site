What is Shadows?

Shadows is a cybernetic, supernatural roleplaying game that uses the Synergy System and is fueled by cinematic, collaborative gameplay. Set in the dystopian near-future of 2099, players create characters to inhabit NYTE City, a megalopolis spanning the entire Eastern Seaboard of the United States.

Together with the GM, players take on every kind of threat NYTE City has to offer — from street punks looking to make a name for themselves, to eldritch horrors trying to consume the world from within.

What is the Synergy System?

The Synergy System is our home-brewed d10 system, built to reward ingenuity over cookie-cutter play — done right, no two games of Shadows feel the same.

A single d10 resolves almost anything a scene calls for: hacking the NET, dispatching enemies, casting a spell, or talking your way out of a holding cell. More on how it works lives in Rules Preview.

How do I make a character?

Character creation is straightforward, with plenty of room to grow as the campaign goes.

  1. Roll for points, based on the campaign's power level.
  2. Assign points to your Stats — this is who you were before power found you.
  3. Select an Archetype (or run baseline Human) — the pivot that sharpens who you're becoming.
  4. Allocate Skill Points, then spend Character Points on Advantages and Disadvantages.

As the campaign progresses, characters earn Improvement Points and Milestones for new Abilities and Powers. Full walkthrough on the Character Creation page.
